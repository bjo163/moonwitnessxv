WHO IS ANGELWITHSHOTGUN?

A GUARDIAN’S PATH THROUGH DARKNESS, TO PROTECT WHAT STILL MATTERS.

AngelWithShotgun is a seeker, protector, and bridge between broken worlds. Walking the edge between shadow and light, they carry both the weight of loss and the fire of a brighter tomorrow. Through action, reflection, and compassion, AngelWithShotgun fights for a more humane world.
