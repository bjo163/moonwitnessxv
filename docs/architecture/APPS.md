# App/runtime contract
At commit `8b6cb118a8`, the repository had six app directories: `apps/radio` (accepted ARSY runtime name, physical rename pending), `apps/dock` Next public, `apps/headquarters` privileged Next, `apps/cockpit` local Vite GUI/process supervision, `apps/aigateway` internal inference, and `apps/sentinel` worker. Since then, M38-07 has added a minimal `apps/skylink` health-only skeleton as the seventh app directory; provider messaging, D18 storage and operator UI remain unimplemented. ECHOLOG (`apps/echolog`) is accepted by [ADR-0007](../decisions/ADR-0007-echolog-storage.md) as a planned eighth app owning D19 with **no tables at all**; its directory and service are not implemented. Compose currently hosts db/radio/Dock/HQ/backup, not eight public deployments.

Proposed LIGHTHOUSE within Dock; HARBOR membership D14, SHADOW editing D07, MoonLab research D05, WORKS order D15/finance D16 and conditional HELIOGRID D17 read-only energy. All use single D00, ARSY/MoonQL, D08 publishing gates and global id/en GUI. Do not add new API/database or SSO per frontend. SKYLINK (D18) and ECHOLOG (D19) are the only accepted exceptions; ECHOLOG owns no tables at all. Protect Cockpit/AI/Sentinel and minimize externally bound ports.
