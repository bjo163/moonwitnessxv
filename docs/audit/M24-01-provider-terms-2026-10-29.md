# M24-01 — Provider terms review (2026-10-29)

This is a dated, source-linked review for planning only. It is not legal advice, a provider-specific reuse approval, or authorization to enable an adapter. No provider adapter is implemented in MoonWitness XV at this review date. The exact provider product, endpoint, fields, and redistribution path must be selected and reviewed before ingestion.

## Decision gate

These sources support a bounded terms inventory, not an implementation decision. Before a provider is enabled, record the chosen product/API and version, source attribution, licence/terms, request quota, freshness and revision semantics, permitted storage and redistribution, and the owner who approved the use. Preserve provider uncertainty and corrections in the observation model. Do not use any feed to issue emergency instructions or claim physical HELIOGRID measurements.

No provider terms are copied into runtime code, no external data were fetched, and no rights clearance is asserted by this review.
