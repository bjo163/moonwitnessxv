## Volunteer Role Framework

### 1. Volunteer Role Cards

#### Role Card 1: Administrative Support Volunteer

**Role Title:** Administrative Support Volunteer  
**Reports To:** Program Director  
**Supervisor:** Volunteer Coordinator  
**Risk Level:** LOW

**Permitted Tasks:**
- Data entry (non-sensitive information only)
- Filing and document organization
- Answering general inquiries (scripted responses only)
- Preparing materials for programs (photocopying, assembling kits)
- General office support under supervision

**Prohibited Tasks:**
- Handling cash or financial transactions
- Accessing beneficiary personal information
- Making program decisions or commitments
- Working alone with beneficiaries
- Transporting beneficiaries
- Accessing restricted funds or financial records

**Screening Requirements:**
- Background check (criminal record, if required by jurisdiction)
- Reference check (2 professional or character references)
- Interview with Volunteer Coordinator
- Agreement to code of conduct

**Training Requirements:**
- Organization mission and values (1 hour)
- Confidentiality and data protection (1 hour)
- Safeguarding awareness (2 hours)
- Task-specific training (2 hours)
- Total: 6 hours before starting

**Supervision Requirements:**
- Direct supervision by staff member at all times
- Weekly check-in with Volunteer Coordinator
- No unsupervised access to beneficiaries or sensitive data

**Safeguarding Boundaries:**
- Never alone with beneficiary (two-adult rule)
- No physical contact beyond handshake (cultural sensitivity training)
- Report any concerns immediately to Safeguarding Lead
- No social media contact with beneficiaries

**Incident Escalation:**
1. Volunteer → Volunteer Coordinator (immediate)
2. Volunteer Coordinator → Program Director (within 24 hours)
3. Program Director → Safeguarding Lead (if safeguarding concern)
4. Safeguarding Lead → External Authorities (if criminal allegation)

#### Role Card 2: Program Support Volunteer

**Role Title:** Program Support Volunteer  
**Reports To:** Program Director  
**Supervisor:** Local Partner Liaison  
**Risk Level:** MEDIUM

**Permitted Tasks:**
- Assisting with program activities under staff supervision
- Distributing supplies to beneficiaries (group settings only)
- Facilitating group activities (with staff present)
- Providing translation services (if qualified)
- Documenting program activities (photos with consent, notes)

**Prohibited Tasks:**
- Working alone with beneficiaries
- Making decisions about beneficiary eligibility
- Handling cash or financial transactions
- Providing counseling or medical advice
- Transporting beneficiaries
- Accessing beneficiary personal information beyond name and contact for program delivery

**Screening Requirements:**
- Enhanced background check (criminal record + child/vulnerable adult registry)
- Reference check (3 references, including one from previous volunteer work)
- Interview with Program Director and Local Partner Liaison
- Skills verification (if translation or specialized support)
- Agreement to code of conduct and safeguarding policy

**Training Requirements:**
- Organization mission and values (1 hour)
- Confidentiality and data protection (2 hours)
- Safeguarding and protection (4 hours)
- Cultural sensitivity and inclusion (2 hours)
- Program-specific training (4 hours)
- Emergency procedures (2 hours)
- Total: 15 hours before starting

**Supervision Requirements:**
- Direct supervision by staff member or Local Partner Liaison
- Never alone with beneficiaries (two-adult rule)
- Daily check-in with supervisor during program activities
- Weekly review with Volunteer Coordinator
- Monthly performance feedback

**Safeguarding Boundaries:**
- Two-adult rule at all times (never alone with beneficiary)
- No physical contact beyond culturally appropriate gestures (training required)
- No gift-giving to beneficiaries without supervisor approval
- No social media contact with beneficiaries
- Report any concerns immediately to Safeguarding Lead
- No transportation of beneficiaries in personal vehicle

**Incident Escalation:**
1. Volunteer → Supervisor (immediate)
2. Supervisor → Program Director (within 4 hours)
3. Program Director → Safeguarding Lead (within 24 hours)
4. Safeguarding Lead → External Authorities (if criminal allegation, immediately)

#### Role Card 3: Skilled Professional Volunteer

**Role Title:** Skilled Professional Volunteer (e.g., medical, legal, counseling)  
**Reports To:** Program Director  
**Supervisor:** Program Director or designated professional staff  
**Risk Level:** HIGH

**Permitted Tasks:**
- Providing professional services within scope of license/certification
- Training staff or other volunteers (if qualified)
- Advising on program design (professional expertise)
- Documenting professional services (per professional standards)

**Prohibited Tasks:**
- Practicing beyond scope of license/certification
- Working alone with beneficiaries (unless professional setting with consent)
- Making program decisions outside professional scope
- Handling cash or financial transactions
- Providing services without proper documentation
- Dual relationships (professional + personal relationship with beneficiary)

**Screening Requirements:**
- Enhanced background check (criminal record + professional license verification)
- Reference check (3 professional references)
- Credential verification (license, certification, insurance)
- Interview with Program Director and professional supervisor
- Agreement to code of conduct, safeguarding policy, and professional ethics

**Training Requirements:**
- Organization mission and values (1 hour)
- Confidentiality and data protection (2 hours)
- Safeguarding and protection (4 hours)
- Professional boundaries and ethics (4 hours)
- Program-specific orientation (4 hours)
- Emergency procedures (2 hours)
- Cultural sensitivity and inclusion (2 hours)
- Total: 19 hours before starting

**Supervision Requirements:**
- Professional supervision by qualified staff member
- Case review meetings (weekly for high-risk cases)
- Documentation review (per professional standards)
- Performance evaluation (quarterly)
- Peer consultation encouraged

**Safeguarding Boundaries:**
- Professional boundaries at all times (no dual relationships)
- Informed consent for all professional services
- Documentation per professional standards
- Mandatory reporting obligations (per professional license)
- No gift-giving to beneficiaries
- No social media contact with beneficiaries
- Report any concerns immediately to Safeguarding Lead

**Incident Escalation:**
1. Volunteer → Professional Supervisor (immediate)
2. Professional Supervisor → Program Director (within 4 hours)
3. Program Director → Safeguarding Lead (within 24 hours)
4. Safeguarding Lead → External Authorities (if criminal allegation or mandatory reporting, immediately)
5. Professional licensing board notified (if professional misconduct)

### 3. Screening and Training Standards

#### 3.1 Screening Levels

**Basic Screening (LOW risk roles):**
- Criminal background check (per jurisdiction requirements)
- 2 reference checks
- Interview with Volunteer Coordinator
- Code of conduct agreement

**Enhanced Screening (MEDIUM risk roles):**
- Enhanced criminal background check (including child/vulnerable adult registry)
- 3 reference checks (including one from previous volunteer work)
- Interview with Program Director and Local Partner Liaison
- Code of conduct and safeguarding policy agreement

**Enhanced + Credential Screening (HIGH risk roles):**
- Enhanced criminal background check
- Professional license/certification verification
- 3 professional reference checks
- Interview with Program Director and professional supervisor
- Code of conduct, safeguarding policy, and professional ethics agreement
- Professional liability insurance verification

#### 3.2 Training Modules

**Core Modules (all volunteers):**
1. Organization mission and values (1 hour)
2. Confidentiality and data protection (1-2 hours)
3. Safeguarding and protection (2-4 hours)

**Specialized Modules (role-specific):**
4. Cultural sensitivity and inclusion (2 hours, MEDIUM/HIGH roles)
5. Program-specific training (2-4 hours)
6. Emergency procedures (2 hours, MEDIUM/HIGH roles)
7. Professional boundaries and ethics (4 hours, HIGH roles)

**Ongoing Training:**
- Annual safeguarding refresher (2 hours)
- Quarterly team meetings (1 hour)
- Task-specific updates as needed

### 4. Supervision Requirements

#### 4.1 Supervision Levels

**Direct Supervision (LOW risk):**
- Staff member present and available
- Weekly check-in with Volunteer Coordinator
- No unsupervised access to sensitive areas

**Enhanced Supervision (MEDIUM risk):**
- Staff member or Local Partner Liaison present at all times
- Two-adult rule (never alone with beneficiary)
- Daily check-in during program activities
- Weekly review with Volunteer Coordinator

**Professional Supervision (HIGH risk):**
- Qualified professional staff member supervising
- Case review meetings (weekly for high-risk cases)
- Documentation review per professional standards
- Quarterly performance evaluation

#### 4.2 Supervision Documentation

**Required Records:**
- Volunteer attendance logs
- Supervision meeting notes
- Performance feedback forms
- Incident reports (if any)
- Training completion certificates

**Retention:** 7 years per CHAR-004 policy

### 5. Prohibited Actions (All Volunteers)

**Absolute Prohibitions:**
1. **Cash handling:** No volunteer may handle cash or financial transactions
2. **Lone working:** No volunteer may be alone with a beneficiary (two-adult rule)
3. **Transportation:** No volunteer may transport beneficiaries in personal vehicle
4. **Unauthorized decisions:** No volunteer may make program decisions outside scope
5. **Gift-giving:** No volunteer may give personal gifts to beneficiaries (organization gifts with supervisor approval only)
6. **Social media contact:** No volunteer may contact beneficiaries via personal social media
7. **Dual relationships:** No volunteer may have personal relationship with beneficiary beyond program scope
8. **Confidentiality breach:** No volunteer may disclose beneficiary personal information
9. **Practicing beyond scope:** No volunteer may provide services beyond training/certification
10. **Retaliation:** No volunteer may retaliate against anyone reporting a concern

**Consequences of Violation:**
- Immediate suspension from volunteer role
- Investigation by Safeguarding Lead
- Possible termination of volunteer relationship
- Referral to external authorities (if criminal allegation)
- Documentation in volunteer record

### 6. Incident Reporting and Appeals

#### 6.1 Incident Types

**Safeguarding Incident:**
- Allegation of abuse, neglect, or exploitation
- Concern about volunteer behavior
- Beneficiary disclosure of harm
- Observation of unsafe situation

**Program Incident:**
- Volunteer injury during program activity
- Beneficiary injury during program activity
- Property damage
- Program disruption

**Confidentiality Breach:**
- Unauthorized disclosure of beneficiary information
- Loss of documents containing personal data
- Data security incident

#### 6.2 Reporting Procedure

**Step 1: Immediate Safety**
- Ensure safety of beneficiary and others
- Provide first aid if needed
- Call emergency services if required

**Step 2: Report to Supervisor**
- Volunteer reports incident to immediate supervisor (verbally and in writing)
- Supervisor documents incident within 4 hours

**Step 3: Escalate to Program Director**
- Supervisor reports to Program Director within 24 hours
- Program Director assesses severity and determines next steps

**Step 4: Safeguarding Lead (if safeguarding concern)**
- Program Director reports to Safeguarding Lead within 24 hours
- Safeguarding Lead initiates investigation

**Step 5: External Authorities (if required)**
- Safeguarding Lead or Program Director contacts external authorities immediately if:
  - Criminal allegation
  - Mandatory reporting obligation
  - Serious harm or risk of harm

**Step 6: Documentation**
- All incidents documented in incident report form
- Reports retained for 7 years per CHAR-004 policy
- Confidential access only (Safeguarding Lead, Program Director, Board/Owner)

#### 6.3 Appeals Process

**Volunteer Appeal:**
1. Volunteer submits written appeal to Program Director within 7 days of decision
2. Program Director reviews appeal and responds within 14 days
3. If unsatisfied, volunteer may appeal to Board/Owner
4. Board/Owner decision is final

**Beneficiary Appeal:**
1. Beneficiary (or advocate) submits complaint to Complaints Officer
2. Complaints Officer investigates and responds within 14 days
3. If unsatisfied, beneficiary may appeal to Safeguarding Lead
4. Safeguarding Lead reviews and responds within 14 days
5. If unsatisfied, beneficiary may contact external authorities

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Volunteer violates prohibited action | Screening, training, supervision, and consequences clearly defined |
| Inadequate screening misses red flag | Enhanced screening for MEDIUM/HIGH risk roles; credential verification for professionals |
