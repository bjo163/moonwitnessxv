# M47 — Frontier research and bounded prototypes

**Purpose:** Thirty bounded research/prototype candidates across existing AI, corpus, research, graph, UX and privacy owners. Start with synthetic or approved fixtures; no automatic production integration or expanded authority.

**Entry conditions:** M21/security and task-level prerequisites; rights, consent and publication gates apply to non-synthetic content.

**Exit gate:** Each leaf ends with stated evidence and a continue/no-go decision; AI remains attributable and reviewable. All stay unchecked.

**Task scope:** 30 leaves map one-to-one to FUT-001..FUT-030. [TODO](../../TODO.md) owns status. The [V5 matrix](../research/unified-100-issue-frontier-v5/ISSUE_MASTER_MATRIX.md) gives the crosswalk; packets link full specs.

## Parallel work

Prototipe sintetis dapat berjalan paralel per hipotesis setelah M21/security dan ENG contracts terpenuhi; jangan integrasikan hasil ke production.

## Known risks

Novelty, model confidence dan manfaat pengguna dapat dibesar-besarkan; gunakan hipotesis, budget, data sintetis, reviewer dan no-go threshold.

## Completion evidence

Close the milestone only when each TODO leaf meets its acceptance criteria, owner review and exact evidence at a pinned SHA; resolve the exit gates above. TODO remains the sole status source.
