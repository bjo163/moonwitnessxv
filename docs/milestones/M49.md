# M49 — HARBOR humanitarian and safeguarding readiness

**Purpose:** Ten research/design tasks within HARBOR/M32 boundaries: privacy-minimizing protocols, accountability, fund controls, volunteer roles, evaluation and safeguarding. No beneficiary intake, outreach, case handling, donation custody or impact claim is authorized.

**Entry conditions:** M32 scope review, named accountable operator and task dependencies; participant contact/sensitive data require separate authority and consent.

**Exit gate:** Protocols include accountability, minimization, complaints and null/harm outcomes as applicable. All stay unchecked.

**Task scope:** 10 leaves map one-to-one to CHAR-001..CHAR-010. [TODO](../../TODO.md) owns status. The [V5 matrix](../research/unified-100-issue-frontier-v5/ISSUE_MASTER_MATRIX.md) gives the crosswalk; packets link full specs.
