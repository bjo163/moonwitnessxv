## ECO-001 — Validate institutional demand for a research-support offer

1. **Identity:** Economy; monetizable value/market validation; P2 / Medium / M / confidence Low / risk Medium; domain 68, common 70; owner: product research; V5-W3; discovery, institution.
2. **Executive:** Learn whether libraries, research groups or educational institutions have a costly research workflow MoonWitness can responsibly support.
3. **Current state:** No institutional buyer, demand, price or revenue was verified; M35 covers a real service/buyer in another scope.
4. **Problem:** Building paid features without a defined buyer and purchasing problem risks mission drift.
5. **Evidence:** TODO M35 and product boundaries R02/R11; interviews not yet conducted.
6. **Value:** Tests a segment distinct from the first M35 service before infrastructure is proposed.
7. **Solution:** Conduct 6–10 consented problem interviews with institutional researchers; test current workflow, procurement constraints and non-solution alternatives.
8. **Architecture:** Research protocol only; no participant data in product databases and no payment flow.
9. **Microtasks:** Select eligible roles; prepare consent script; interview; anonymize themes; seek disconfirming cases; publish go/no-go.
10. **Acceptance:** At least three independent respondents describe the same costly job and current workaround; otherwise document insufficient/negative evidence.
11. **DoD:** No identifying quotes without consent; sample and selection bias stated.
12. **Tests:** Review research protocol for leading questions and anonymization.
13. **Dependencies:** Reconcile M35-01 scope; enables ECO-002.
14. **Risks:** Small sample and institutional courtesy bias; no demand claim from interest alone.
15. **Metrics:** Repeated problem themes, workaround cost ranges and procurement blocker frequency.
16. **Estimate:** 5–8 researcher days; interview incentives only with approval.
17. **Rollback:** Delete raw notes on schedule; retain only consented/anonymized findings.
18. **Synergy:** Could support knowledge/research sustainability without gating public content.
19. **Anti-overlap:** Not validating the M35 physical/service pilot or making a sales offer.
20. **Outcome:** Evidence to stop, refine or test one institutional offer.
21. **Future:** Pricing study only if repeated need is established.
22. **Closure evidence:** Approved protocol, anonymized results, limitations and decision.
