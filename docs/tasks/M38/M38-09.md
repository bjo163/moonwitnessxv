**EXECUTABLE LEAF, NOT VERIFIED** · Priority P0 · Difficulty D4. [Canonical task state](../../../TODO.md) · [Milestone](../../milestones/M38.md).

## Objective and grounded context
`packages/whatsapp/src/client.ts` today contains Hub business logic: `buildDefaultHandler()` calls `/api/v1/views/nowPlaying`, `/stations`, `/requests`, `/monitor_signals` and `/orm/unilex/terms`, plus brand copy, and imports `HUB_ORIGIN_DEFAULT` from core. A transport package that dials the Hub cannot live in a standalone service. The bot must move to its consumer.

## Boundaries
- Paths: `packages/skylink-whatsapp/*` (moved from `packages/whatsapp`), `apps/radio/src/channels/whatsapp.ts` (receives the radio bot), root manifests and tsconfig references.
- Pin `@whiskeysockets/baileys@7.0.0-rc14` exactly; document `legacy 6.7.24` as the rollback.
- The package must contain no Hub URL, no brand copy, no domain commands; receive behaviour arrives through an injected handler.
- Credentials stay in the ignored auth directory; the DB stores a reference only.
