# M41-08 — Implement human-approval pause and resume

**EXECUTABLE LEAF, NOT VERIFIED** · Priority P1 · Difficulty D4. [TODO](../../../TODO.md) owns status.

## Objective

Suspend consequential/restricted tool calls before their side effects and resume only through the trusted ARSY/Headquarters/100CENTER approval contract.

## Boundaries

1. Depend on M41-05 and M41-07; require applicable M20 governance and M37 identity gates before real authorization integration.
2. Bind every request to run ID, tool ID/version, validated argument hash, authority/scope, expiry and the identity of the authorized approver.
3. Any changed argument, expired decision, wrong run/tool/version, duplicate decision or insufficient approver permission must invalidate the approval.
4. Store the canonical decision in its existing owner service. Do not make LUMEN or ORBIT the approval database/UI.
5. The model cannot approve itself; natural-language text such as “approved” is not an approval token.
6. Waiting releases the worker lease; resume goes through a new authorized claim.

## Acceptance evidence

- Positive: a pending tool remains unexecuted until a matching valid approval arrives; then the exact approved request can resume.
- Negative: stale, replayed, changed-argument, wrong-actor, denied and expired approvals never execute the tool.
- Validate audit correlation and safe behavior when the approval owner is unavailable.

## Required report

Record relevant M20/M37 gates, test output, approval binding format, changed paths, reviewer and exact SHA.
