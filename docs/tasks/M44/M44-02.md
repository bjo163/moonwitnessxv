## Objective

Bind each hostname in ADR-0016 to its service in Coolify, issue one certificate per host, and make `www` a single-hop redirect — without touching DNS records outside the documented set.

Deviations and open rows:

1. **`www` is redirected at the Cloudflare edge, not in Coolify.** Coolify's compose domains cannot express a redirect; each zone carries one wildcard Single Redirect (301, `${1}` path capture, query preserved). Recorded in audit §9.2.
2. **Skylink has no container.** The compose service sits behind `profiles: ["skylink"]` and Coolify does not honour `COMPOSE_PROFILES` ([coollabsio/coolify#6395](https://github.com/collabsio/coolify/issues/6395)); `SKYLINK_SERVICE_KEY` is also unset, and SKYLINK refuses to boot in production without it. No certificate is issued to the router until a backend exists.
3. `http://` → `https://` upgrade was not observed through a clean resolver; this host's resolver still answers `a-l-l-a-h.com` with the old parking IP.
