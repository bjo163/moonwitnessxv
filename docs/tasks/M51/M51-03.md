## Objective

Add D20 organization, RADIUS, NAS/network and session/accounting records through `@moonwitness/moonql-isp`, with custom ARSY endpoints and D00 scoped operator grants.

## Scope and boundaries

- Keep all durable D20 data in the existing MoonQL/Postgres owner.
- Disable generic REST for tenant-sensitive records; enforce tenant grants and audit on every read/write and background action.
- Store only NAS secret references. Do not add D15/D16 invoice/payment tables in D20.

## Acceptance and evidence

- Additive schema, typed API/SDK and synthetic fixtures cover tenant organization, NAS, RADIUS profile/user binding, session/accounting and registered monitor references.
- Positive tests prove an authorized grant can read/write only its tenant; negative tests reject missing, expired and cross-tenant grants and hide private fields.
- No model/API response contains a NAS secret or cross-domain customer/invoice/payment copy.
