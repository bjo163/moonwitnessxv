**EXECUTABLE LEAF** · P1/D4 · Depends on M35-01..M35-04, M51-03 · [TODO status](../../../TODO.md) · [Milestone](../../milestones/M51.md).

## Objective

Map MWX-ISP customer, package, subscription, invoice, manual payment and enforcement behavior to D15/D16 and D20 without duplicate canonical records.

## Scope and boundaries

- D15 owns customers, commercial packages, subscriptions and invoices; D16 owns manual payment/receipt and reconciliation; D20 owns technical access binding/enforcement.
- Preserve integer IDR, invoice idempotency, partial/full payment, due/grace rules and manual-suspension precedence.
- No online payment gateway, custody, public customer portal or real-money operation without M35 approval.

## Acceptance and evidence

- Synthetic end-to-end fixtures prove invoice generation is idempotent and payment replay creates no duplicate receipt or access action.
- Positive tests show settled payment restores only billing-suspended access after policy checks; negative tests preserve manual suspension and deny cross-tenant payment updates.
- D15/D16 records remain the only customer/invoice/payment sources.

## Verification and rollback

Run owning D15/D16 and D20 integration tests with synthetic data only. Roll back the integration consumer while retaining auditable canonical transactions.
